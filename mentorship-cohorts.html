Mentorship Cohorts

Join focused, time-bound groups with weekly milestones and mentor guidance.

Overview

Cohorts run for 6–10 weeks with curated curricula, project work, and regular check-ins. Expect a blend of theory, practice, and peer collaboration.

Tracks

Popular tracks include Full-Stack Web, Data Structures & Algorithms, AI/ML, AR/VR, and Blockchain. Tracks vary based on mentor availability.

Schedule

Sessions are usually held twice a week with an optional weekend sync. Detailed schedule is shared post-selection.

How to Apply

Applications open periodically. Submit interest via the Contact form on the Home page, mentioning your preferred track and experience level.

Contact

Questions? Use the Home page Contact section or email via the announcement channels when the cohort is announced.
